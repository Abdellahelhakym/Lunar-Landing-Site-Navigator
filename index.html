About

Method

Visibility is evaluated hourly by comparing target elevation with the local horizon. The score is the weighted mean of Sun and Earth visibility. Blackout is when neither target is visible.

Assumptions

Sunlight supports power estimates; Earth visibility supports direct communications. The south pole is difficult because the Sun stays low, shadows can be long, and Earth is visible only during part of the lunar cycle. Coordinates use East-positive longitude and time values use UT.

Limits

A flat horizon is used when terrain data is absent. Site altitude is simplified to 0 km above the ellipsoid unless specified. The Sun is a point source; atmosphere and refraction are not modeled.
